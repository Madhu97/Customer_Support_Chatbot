# Online Shop FAQ

## Orders

### How can I check my order status?

Sign in to your account, open **My Orders**, and select the order to view
its current status.

### Can I cancel an order?

Orders can be cancelled only before they enter the shipping process.
If the cancellation option is unavailable, contact human support.

---

## Shipping

### How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping normally takes 3–5 business days after the order ships.

### Can I track my shipment?

Yes. Open **My Orders**, select the shipped order, and use the tracking
information shown there.

---

## Returns

### What is the return policy?

Eligible products may be returned within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unused and in their original condition.

### How do I start a return?

Open **My Orders**, select the eligible order, and choose the return option.

---

## Payments

### What payment methods are supported?

The shop accepts major credit and debit cards and the payment methods
displayed during checkout.

### Was I charged twice?

If you see duplicate charges, first check whether one charge is still
pending. If both charges settle, contact human support.

---

## Products

### How can I find product information?

Product descriptions, specifications, and availability are shown on
each product page.

---

## Account

### How do I change my account information?

Sign in and open your account settings to update the available profile
information.

---

## Privacy

### How is my information handled?

The shop uses customer information to provide its services and manage
orders. For privacy questions not answered here, contact human support.
